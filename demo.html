Test the RevuLens extension text-selection workflow directly in your browser.

How to test with RevuLens:

  1. Make sure the backend is running: uvicorn backend.app.main:app --port 8000.
  2. Ensure the unpacked extension is loaded in chrome://extensions/. (If testing from local file:// URL, click "Details" on the extension and enable "Allow access to file URLs").
  3. Select (highlight) any text inside the review cards below using your mouse cursor.
  4. Click the floating "RevuLens: Analyze Review" badge that appears over your selection.
  5. Observe the classification (Likely Genuine or Potentially Deceptive) and the word-level SHAP color highlights!
